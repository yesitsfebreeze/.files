These are the checks that no script could perform — timing you have to feel,
rendering you have to look at, a gesture you have to make. They lived in
`gates/manual/wave*.md` until those files were deleted on 2026-08-31.

**None of the open ones below has been run.** They are kept because the record
of what was never checked is worth more than a blank space, and because each one
names a specific way the configuration could be wrong in a way nothing else
would catch.

<Callout type="warn" title="This is a list of unknowns, not a to-do list">
Nothing here is known to be broken. Nothing here is known to work either. If a
behaviour on this page matters to you, the check beside it is how you find out.
</Callout>
