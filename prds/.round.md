## Decided
- **Six runs of one harness, and every red but one was a check that could not
  discriminate** — not a defect in the thing under test. The rule is
  [`an-absence-assertion-needs-a-positive-precondition`](memos/an-absence-assertion-needs-a-positive-precondition-or-it-is-green-on-a-blank-screen.md):
  never assert an absence without asserting the screen exists.
- **R1 of the doctor node was reversed rather than implemented.** Its premise
  was false, and sweeping the eight would have deleted a true record to green a
  check — option 2's failure mode, reached through the half that looked
  mechanical.
- **A requirement that contradicts an invariant gets neither obeyed nor
  ignored.** R7 named `install.sh`; §4 IS `chezmoi apply`, which a gate pins as
  last. I1 — "apply-time, not launch-time" — is what a `run_after` satisfies.
- **The frontmost guard earned itself before the first check ran.** With a
  shared bundle id macOS activates by bundle: `set frontmost` on the probe
  reported success and left the USER's window frontmost. Unguarded, those
  keystrokes were going into a live session.
