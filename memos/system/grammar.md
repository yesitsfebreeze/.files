# grammar

A memo with `kind: grammar` records one name this repo uses in its own way: a
coinage, a word borrowed from another field and given a local job, or a plain
word narrowed to one meaning here. One name, one memo, so a reader meeting the
word in code or in the record has exactly one place to land.

The body carries the whole claim, in this order: **what the word names here**,
**where it first occurred**, **why it is that word**, and the near names as
wikilinks. A name whose choice nothing recorded gets the reading it is used
with and no invented rationale.

The word is the body's `# ` heading, not the leaf: leaves are unique across
`memos/` and most of these words are already held by a declaration or a
routine. So the file is `<word>-grammar.md`.
