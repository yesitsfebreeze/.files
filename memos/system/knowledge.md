# knowledge

What the project is: how a piece of config works, what a contract holds,
what a measurement read. Trust: true until the config changes, and the memo
is fixed in the same change that changes it. The body is one claim, its
evidence and why it matters — a table, a walk-through, a list that proves
the claim — under no headings of its own.
