# decision

Something settled that would otherwise be re-argued. Frontmatter carries
`date:` and `status: decided | refuted | unmeasured`; the body carries
exactly the headings `## Decision`, `## Why`, `## Consequences`. Trust: it
stands until superseded by a memo that names it, or until the config that
implements it lands and proves it false. Superseding adds a memo naming the
older one; it never deletes the sentence it corrects. An answered
[[question]] is rewritten in this shape.
